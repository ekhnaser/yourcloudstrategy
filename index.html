The Book

Cloud is not the strategy. Business outcomes are.

A cloud program can meet every migration target and still leave its executives asking: What did the business gain?

In Cloud Is Not the Strategy, Elias Khnaser shows how to answer that question before the investment begins. He presents a practical method for connecting business priorities to cloud decisions, implementation, and measurable results.

Follow Northstar Manufacturing Group, a fictional manufacturer navigating acquisitions, fragmented systems, and AI ambitions. Its choices reveal where cloud creates value, when modernization is worth the cost, and why leaving a workload where it is can be the right call.

From executive sponsorship and financial accountability to security, resilience, and provider dependency, Khnaser links the decisions leaders make to the capabilities their teams must deliver.

For business leaders, CIOs, and architects who want to turn cloud’s potential into competitive advantage.

About the Author

Elias Khnaser

Elias Khnaser is a technology strategist, author, and former Gartner Research Vice President who helps technology leaders connect cloud investment to business strategy. His work focuses on the decisions that shape enterprise transformation: where to invest, which capabilities to build, and how to turn technology choices into measurable business outcomes.

During seven years at Gartner, Elias researched and advised organizations on private, hybrid, and public cloud computing. Today, he advises enterprises and technology vendors on cloud strategy, edge computing, and data center transformation. He brings an analyst’s rigor and a practical perspective to complex decisions, making technical concepts clear and their business implications actionable.

Elias has authored or co-authored six books, developed dozens of video training courses, and written hundreds of articles for publications including Forbes, InformationWeek, and Virtualization Review. His latest book, Cloud Is Not the Strategy, presents a practical approach to connecting cloud decisions with business priorities, execution, and results.

A frequent keynote speaker, Elias has presented at Gartner Symposium, Catalyst, and IT Infrastructure, Operations & Cloud Strategies conferences, as well as Citrix Synergy and other technology events. He also hosts Let’s Talk Shop, featuring conversations with technology executives and industry leaders about cloud, AI infrastructure, and the decisions shaping enterprise technology.
