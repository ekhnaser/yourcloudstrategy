About the book

The business has a strategy. Technology must be capable of executing it.

In Cloud Is Not the Strategy, Elias Khnaser connects business outcomes to cloud adoption, implementation, and maturity through a practical, business-first methodology.

These companion resources help you apply that method to your own organization. Use them as a starting point for the conversations and decisions that matter.
